[Podbharati](https://www.podbharati.com/) (पॉडभारती) is **India's first Hindi podzine** — a podcast magazine launched in April 2007, aimed at Hindi-speaking audiences in India and abroad. Its debut show, now called *Podbharati Classic*, provided comprehensive coverage of Indian language blogging, technology, current affairs, and the entertainment industry — entirely in Hindi, at a time when Hindi podcasting did not exist as a category.

Podbharati was originally co-founded and co-hosted by **Debashish Chakrabarty** and **Shashi Singh**. The website, logo, banners, and artwork (both old and new) were designed and built by Debashish. Since May 2020, when the podcast moved to its present home at **Podbharati.com**, Debashish has run the venture independently. 

{{<card title="New in 2025" image="/images/books/podcasting-guide-cover.jpg" imageWidth="250px" imageAlign="right">}}
Long before podcasting became mainstream in India, Debashish was already fascinated by the power of audio storytelling. An avid listener of All India Radio and an active quizmaster and master of ceremonies during his school and college years, stepping into podcasting felt like a natural progression.

In early 2007, he launched Podbharati, becoming one of India's early independent podcasters. At a time when podcasting was virtually unknown in the country, he invested in professional recording equipment, experimented with the medium, and spent years learning every aspect of podcast creation—from recording and editing to publishing, audience building, and promotion. Along the way, he also established himself as a voice-over artist, further deepening his understanding of audio production.

Believing that knowledge is most valuable when shared, Debashish distilled nearly two decades of experience in podcasting & voice-over space into **"पॉडकास्टिंग कैसे करें?"**, a comprehensive Hindi guide for both aspiring and experienced podcasters. The book covers everything from choosing the right equipment and recording on a budget to growing an audience, monetising a podcast, and leveraging modern AI tools to create better content.

## A Platform With Many Lives

Podbharati's journey across platforms is itself a document of the evolution of digital audio hosting in India:

{{< timeline >}}

{{< timeline-item date="2007" title="Launch on Blip.tv" icon="📺" >}}
Podbharati's earliest episodes were hosted on [Blip.tv](https://web.archive.org/web/20110716100507/http://www.blip.tv/), one of the few platforms at the time that could host large audio and video files. The original domain was *Podbharti.com* (note the missing 'a'), purchased by Shashi.
{{< /timeline-item >}}

{{< timeline-item date="2007–2012" title="Podbazaar Era" icon="📺" >}}
Hosting moved to [Podbazaar.com](https://web.archive.org/web/20080914180907/http://www.podbazaar.com:80/view/126100789566374041), a pioneering Indian podcast hosting platform, founded by California-based  [Sujatha Jagannathan](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sujathaj/).

On Podbazaar, Podbharati hosted two feeds:
- [**Podbharati** (original)](http://web.archive.org/web/20110929073545/http://www.podbazaar.com/view/126100789566374041) — **13,800+ downloads**
- [**Podbharati Light**](http://web.archive.org/web/20110907004546/http://www.podbazaar.com/view/126100789566374065) (abridged) — **7,000+ downloads**

These numbers are significant given the dismal internet speeds of the era and the fact that audio streaming had not yet arrived.
{{< /timeline-item >}}

{{< timeline-item date="2012" title="Domain Expiry & File Recovery" icon="🗃️" >}}
When *Podbharti.com* expired in 2012 and Podbazaar.com also shut down the same year, and no proper backups, many original MP3 file recordings of our podcast disappeared. Using his personal archives and the [Internet Archive's Wayback Machine](https://web.archive.org/), Debashish retrieved the original files and moved them to [SoundCloud](https://soundcloud.com/podbharati) thus preserving the historical record of India's first Hindi podcast.
{{< /timeline-item >}}

{{< timeline-item date="2020" title="Relaunch as Podbharati.com" icon="📖"  current="true">}}
In May 2020, Debashish registered the new domain **Podbharati.com** and relaunched the venture as a full podcast platform — no longer a single show but a home for multiple Hindi podcast series. New shows are hosted on Anchor.fm (now Spotify for Creators), with [YouTube channel](https://youtube.com/@podbharati) and [Instagram channels](https://instagram.com/podbharati/) added in July 2020 featuring podcast episodes, videos, and Shorts.
{{< /timeline-item >}}

{{< /timeline >}}

## Podcast Parikrama

In September 2021, Debashish launched **[Podcast Parikrama](https://parikrama.substack.com/)** — a Hindi Substack newsletter covering the world of audio and podcasting globally. It now has over **700 subscribers** and is one of the few dedicated Hindi-language resources for the podcasting community in India.
