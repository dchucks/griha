[Buno Kahani](http://bunokahani.blogspot.com/) (बुनो कहानी, Hindi for *“weave a story”*) was a collaborative fiction experiment I ran on Blogger between 2005 and 2007. Instead of one author owning a story from start to finish, a group of Hindi bloggers took turns: one would begin a story, another would continue it — with no prior discussion of plot, direction, or length. 

## How It Worked

Every story lived as a series of numbered chapters, with no single author in control of where it was headed. A blogger would pick up wherever the previous chapter left off, free to twist the plot, introduce a character, or change the tone entirely. That constraint — writing blind, with no say over what came before and no idea what would come after — is what gave the results their suspense and unpredictability, for readers and contributing writers alike.

## The Stories

Six stories took shape through this back-and-forth, spanning crime, romance, and nostalgia:

- **अपराध बोध: भयानक रातें** (*Crime Guilt: Terrifying Nights*) — a chapter-by-chapter crime story built on guilt and psychological tension
- **कारे कजरारे: परिवर्तन** / **नई उड़ान** (*Kare Kajrare: Change* / *New Flight*) — a multi-chapter romantic and social drama
- **मरीचिका: यादें** (*Mirage: Memories*) — a reflective, memory-driven narrative
- **भैंसिया का नैनमटक्का** — a chapter with a distinctly rural, folk flavour
