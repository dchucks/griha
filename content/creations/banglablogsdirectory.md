Bangla Blogs Directory was a directory of Bengali blogs to enable easy discovery of the best of the Bengali blogs all around the globe and help readers rate them. There was a category word cloud, and search facility available as well. It saw over 10 lacs hits out (clicks to visit the blogs). It was active from 2006 to 2014.

There is a rarely updated Blogger.com version of the website at [https://banglablogs.blogspot.com/](https://banglablogs.blogspot.com/) that you may like to visit.
